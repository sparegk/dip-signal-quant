# Proposed focus: large-cap tech and AI

**Status: proposal only. No subgroup results calculated. Not a pre-registration.**

**Question:** Does frozen DipSignal V1 behave differently in large technology
companies than in other sectors?

**Why:** This is a useful area of interest to study. Interest in AI is not evidence
that its stocks offer a better trading edge.

## Before testing

1. Define a technology group using an independent sector classification and record
   its source/date. The existing OEF-derived requested universe is a starting
   point; retain exclusions and label its survivorship bias.
2. Treat an AI-related group separately. Technology is not synonymous with AI.
   Choose and document an independent industry/exposure rule before looking at
   returns. Do not pick companies because their past results look attractive.
3. Keep the other eligible sectors as the comparison. Report sample sizes,
   exclusions, losing stocks, yearly results and concentration for every group.
4. Register the exact membership, dates, comparisons and interpretation criteria
   before calculation. Keep V1, horizons, exits and costs unchanged.

## What the result could tell us

The historical samples have already been inspected. Any new split of those samples
is **exploratory**, not fresh out-of-sample proof. A stronger pooled average could
reflect sector exposure, volatility or a few stocks rather than signal value.

Genuine future evidence must follow a separately registered collection/review
protocol. Do not change the existing 95-name paper archive to favor this theme or
peek at accumulating performance. No purchases, new data acquisition, new backtest
or change to the archive is included in this proposal.
